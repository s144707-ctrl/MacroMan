About Us

Our Story

MacrOman started with a simple problem: popular tracking apps like MyFitnessPal and Cronometer have huge food databases, but local Omani and Gulf dishes are often missing or have inaccurate values. Logging a plate of shuwa, harees or majboos should not mean guessing. We built MacrOman so people in Oman can track the food they actually eat.

Our Logo

Our logo shows a figure pushing a red apple up a steep hill. Improving your health is rarely easy — it takes effort and patience, one step at a time. The journey is hard, but the result is sweet.

Our Mission

  1. Cover local Omani and Gulf dishes with accurate macros.
  2. Make logging meals fast and simple.
  3. Help users set realistic goals and follow their progress.
  4. Work smoothly on phones, tablets and computers.

Who It Is For

  • Gym-goers who track protein and calories
  • Students who want to eat healthier
  • Anyone trying to understand what they eat
